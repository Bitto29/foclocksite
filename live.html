Study Suggestions
Suggested Routine for Now
Pomodoro Technique
Study 25 minutes, 5-minute break, repeat. After 4 cycles take a 20-minute break. This keeps your brain fresh and prevents burnout.
Spaced Repetition
Review at intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 21 days. This method multiplies long-term memory retention dramatically.
Active Recall
After reading, close the book and write everything you remember. Testing yourself is far more effective than re-reading.
Environment Design
Dedicate a specific spot only for studying. Your brain will learn to associate that space with focus, making it easier to start.
Two-Minute Start Rule
Commit to studying for just 2 minutes. Once started, continuing becomes effortless. Starting is always the hardest part.
Subject Rotation
Alternate between subjects each session. Interleaving improves both learning and retention.

How to Use Foc Lock
Starting a Session
Tap the play button in the header or the big Start button on Home. Pick your subject and timer mode (15, 20, 25 min, custom, or no timer). Choose whether fullscreen lock is on, then tap Start.
App Lock Feature
During a session, Foc Lock monitors if you try to leave. With fullscreen lock on, it shows a return banner if you leave the tab. With it off, the session runs normally so you can study in another tab.
Pausing a Session
Tap the pause button. You must solve a simple math problem to pause — this prevents impulsive breaks. While paused, you'll get return notifications every 2 minutes.
Earning XP & Rewards
You earn 1 XP per minute studied. Complete sessions to unlock achievements and level up. Check Rewards for your level, XP bar, and achievement badges.
Setting Reminders
Go to More → Reminders. Add a time and label. The app sends a browser notification at that time daily to remind you to start a session.
Session Music
During a session, use the bottom music bar. Choose ambient sounds, Lo-Fi radio, or Songs radio. Music plays while the timer runs.
Progress Tracking
The Progress tab shows a bar chart of daily study time, a breakdown by subject, and your full session history.
