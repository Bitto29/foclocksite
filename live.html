Study Suggestions
Suggested Routine for Now
Pomodoro Technique
Study 25 minutes, 5-minute break, repeat. After 4 cycles take a 20-minute break. This keeps your brain fresh and prevents burnout.
Spaced Repetition
Review at intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 21 days. This method multiplies long-term memory retention dramatically.
Active Recall
After reading, close the book and write everything you remember. Testing yourself is far more effective than re-reading.
Environment Design
Dedicate a specific spot only for studying. Your brain will learn to associate that space with focus, making it easier to start.
Two-Minute Start Rule
Commit to studying for just 2 minutes. Once started, continuing becomes effortless. Starting is always the hardest part.
Subject Rotation
Alternate between subjects each session instead of studying one subject for hours. Interleaving improves both learning and retention.

How to Use Foc Lock
Starting a Session
Tap the play button in the header or the big Start button on Home. Pick your subject and timer mode (15 min, 20 min, custom, or no timer). Hit Start and the app enters fullscreen lock mode.
App Lock Feature
During a session, Foc Lock monitors if you try to leave. If you switch apps or minimize, a fullscreen return banner appears immediately pushing you back. The Wake Lock API also keeps the screen on.
Pausing a Session
Tap the pause button during a session. You must correctly solve a simple math problem to pause. While paused, the app sends you return notifications every 2 minutes until you resume.
Earning XP & Rewards
You earn 1 XP per minute studied. Complete sessions to unlock achievements and level up. Check the Rewards screen for your level, XP bar, and achievement badges.
Setting Reminders
Go to More > Reminders. Add a time and label. The app will send a browser notification at that exact time each day reminding you to start a session.
Session Music
During a session, swipe the bottom music bar. Choose ambient sounds (Rain, White Noise, Forest, Ocean), Lo-Fi radio, or Bengali band music. Music continues playing while the timer runs.
Progress Tracking
The Progress tab shows a bar chart of daily study time (weekly or monthly), a breakdown by subject, and your full session history with timestamps.
